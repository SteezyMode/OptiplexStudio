## Historical research and experiments

Documents about remote workflows, Wolf, containers, hosted services and earlier
product directions record past experiments. They do not describe current Studio
functionality or impose current runtime requirements. Research notes also include
third-party reference links; they are not Studio service integrations.

## Documentation maintenance

Keep CURRENT STATUS limited to verified behavior in the checked-out source and
clearly identify pending validation. Keep personal service URLs, machine-specific
hostnames and environment-specific paths out of current product documentation.
